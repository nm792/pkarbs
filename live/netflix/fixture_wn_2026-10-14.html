Want to know what’s popular on Netflix daily? We track the top 10 TV series and movies worldwide, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and other countries.

Netflix introduced its top 10 feature in early 2020, enabling us to see what’s trending. This page shows what’s popular on Netflix right now and allows you to go back to see what was trending on any given day globally or in select countries.
