**The one item that matters most.** Whether the weekly "hit" contracts can be bought at decision time, in size, before they reprice. The print test cannot see this (8,264 of 8,575 strikes had no taker-buy print in the hour before the open), and no historical book exists. The only way to measure it is the decision-time book snapshots now scheduled for every weekend (Sun 22:40Z and 23:06Z, Mon 14:20Z).

## What was run (locked rule `LOCKED-RULE-touch-gap-2026-11-22.md`)
- Universe: 58 Polymarket events on underlyings that have a Hyperliquid xyz perp, found by 129 public-search queries (can miss low-volume events; stated), plus 13 missing weekly events counted and excluded. Kalshi: 16,775 series fetched, keyword-screened (analysis only).
- Weekly test: 16 tickers x 29 Mondays (4 May-16 Nov) = 464 events, 451 found, 8,575 resolved strikes; perp = last 1-hour Hyperliquid candle closed by T (stocks 09:20 NY Monday, metals and gas 17:50 NY Sunday), close = last Yahoo daily close; candidate = strike already crossed by the perp (margin 0.2% stocks, 0.5% metals/gas); entry = taker-buy prints of the YES token in the 60 minutes before T, $200 cap per print (FLOOR). Result: `result_touch.json`.
- Can-fail tests, all pass: `test_pscanlib.py` (book order: best ask is the lowest price, not the first element; dead bucket; strike parsing; gap logic; capacity cap; perp depth), `test_snaplib.py` (outcome/token pairing by label, NO never derived from YES, missing book excluded), `test_touchlib.py` (planted edge passes with CI and placebo; zero-edge panels: pass rule fires in 1 of 40; no look-ahead on candles and prints; dead and no-print counted; last_print), `test_universe.py`, `test_ocbooks_depth.py`.
- **Plausibility check (committed with the result).** First candle pull was empty (451 of 451 cells): the analysis printed `no_candle: 451` and zero cells, which I treated as implausible, found the cause (Hyperliquid keeps 1-minute history only for recent days; 5-minute only about 17 days) and recorded it as Amendments 1 and 2 before the amended numbers. One n = 1 line printed during the 5-minute attempt is disclosed in Amendment 2. Final run: 451 cells, 258 candidates, 226 strikes with a usable print, 8,264 NOPRINT, 85 DEAD. Volumes: all 58 events complete (no incomplete pagination); WTI monthly $48.7k/day over 3,744 trades is plausible.
- Limits: Pyth spot vs the futures used for the last close vs the xyz perp is a basis; the 1-hour perp price is up to 20-50 minutes older than T; prints are not quotes; stale-price reference uses prints up to many hours old (median 4.8 h); 29 weekends.
