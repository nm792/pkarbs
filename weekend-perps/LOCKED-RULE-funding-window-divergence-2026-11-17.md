# LOCKED RULE: how far does the perp move away from its index/oracle inside a funding window, how long does it last, and does it revert by the next settlement? (17 Nov 2026, written before any number of this test was computed)

Origin: Nick, 17 Nov 01:00Z: what matters for divergence is the movement between the funding reset windows. Hyperliquid settles funding every hour (xyz, flx, km dexes); Polymarket perps settle every 1 h (instrument field funding_interval = 1h). Mechanics read so far (docs for the trade.xyz weekend session were not reachable from this container, so this comes from search summaries of the official docs and from the API): funding = premium-driven, premium = (impact mid / oracle) - 1 averaged over the hour; xyz scales funding by 0.5 (flx 0.0, i.e. NO funding anchor on flx); mark/oracle on closed sessions are set by the deployer's off-hours method with discovery bounds around the last external price.

## Data (fixed)
A. Hourly history: Hyperliquid fundingHistory (premium = hourly average of (impact mid - oracle)/oracle, fundingRate) for every xyz, flx and km perp with at least 60 days of records. This is the only history the venue publishes; intra-hour paths are NOT available historically.
B. Live intra-hour sample, taken once, today: every 5 s for 30 minutes, mark, oracle and mid from metaAndAssetCtxs (Hyperliquid) and mark, index and mid from /v1/info/tickers (Polymarket), for the fixed set: Hyperliquid xyz GOLD, SILVER, CL, BRENTOIL, SP500, XYZ100, NVDA, EUR (liquid), COPPER, PLATINUM, PALLADIUM, URNM, BIRD, flx OIL, COPPER, GAS, SILVER, km BMNR, RTX (thin); Polymarket SP500, NAS100, GOLD, SILVER, WTIOIL, BRENTOIL, NVDA, MU, AMD, TSLA.
Liquidity tiers = terciles of open interest in USD at analysis time among the A symbols (B symbols labelled by the same cut-offs).
Session class of an hour (UTC): weekend = Fri 22:00 to Sun 22:00; weekday-US = Mon-Fri 13:00-21:00; weekday-other = remaining weekday hours. (Commodity and FX perps use the same classes, as a simplification.)

## Metrics (all reported, per tier x session class)
1. Size: median, 90th and 99th percentile of |premium| in bp; share of hours with |premium| >= 25 bp and >= 50 bp.
2. Persistence: among hours with |premium| >= 25 bp, the share where the NEXT hour's premium has the same sign and |premium| >= half of this hour's ("not reverted by the next settlement"); AR(1) coefficient of the premium; median run length (hours) of same-sign |premium| >= 25 bp.
3. Funding transferred: mean |fundingRate| per hour in bp, and mean funding received by the side that fades the divergence.
4. Reversion trade (single, mechanical): at the end of any hour h with |premium_h| >= 50 bp, take the position AGAINST the sign of the premium (short the perp if it trades above the oracle) at the close of the 1 h trade candle of hour h, exit at the close of hour h+1; P&L = position x (close_{h+1} / close_h - 1) + funding received for hour h+1 (rate x position sign) - costs. Costs = 2 x (9 bp fee + max(3 x live half-spread, 1 bp)) with live half-spreads from l2Book at analysis time. Reported per tier x session class with weekend-cluster bootstrap 95% CI (calendar-week clusters, 10,000 resamples, seed 1).
5. Intra-hour (sample B only): per symbol and per hour-window, the maximum |mid - oracle| and |mark - oracle| in bp, and the time (seconds) for |mid - oracle| to fall back below 5 bp after it exceeded 25 bp (if it ever does within the 30 min); share of exceedances that revert before the next top of the hour.

## Decision rule
"Material divergence" in a cell: >= 5% of hours with |premium| >= 25 bp. "Persistent": >= 50% not reverted by the next settlement. "Tradable reversion": metric 4 mean net > 0 with the 95% CI lower bound > 0 AND n >= 30 events over >= 8 calendar weeks. Anything else is reported as "no tradable reversion"; sample B is descriptive only (30 minutes, one session, no claim beyond what it shows). No threshold may change after the first number is seen; any new idea needs a new rule file.
