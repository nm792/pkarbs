Null simulation (feeds/sim_ps_cs2_null.py, checker, 6000 simulations per row, same `cell_t`, 12 looks, N_MAX 60, >= 12 matches, any-look pass at 2.60/sqrt(n/60)):
false-pass rate 0.10% to 0.63% across arrivals of 3, 5, 8 and 15 matches per day, normal and heavy-tailed (80/20 mixture) per-match d. Target: below 0.93% (one-sided alpha 0.0093). It does not model the real placebo/selection noise; it assumes per-match d iid with mean 0.
Known limit of the rule itself: the lead statistic is censored (events without an onset in +-60 s are dropped) and placebo times are random-sign, so d measures price-reaction timing minus feed delay, not an ordinary difference of means on the same events. That is the PREREG's statistic; I implemented it as written.
