# PRE-REGISTRATION: Goalserve esports odds vs Polymarket price, locked rerun v2 (non-onset). Author: Goalserve thread. 17 Nov 2026 (lock by 18 Nov, before any 18-24 Nov data is read)

**Goal.** Find out, on new data, whether Goalserve esports in-play odds (bookmaker not identified: `bm` empty, vendor docs say bet365) move before the Polymarket price. Benchmark is always the Polymarket price (feeds/STANDARD-feed-latency-test.md v1).
**Nick approval.** Card "Approve the locked Goalserve odds rerun on 18 to 24 Nov data?" answered "Yes, lock and run it", 17 Nov 22:15:13Z.
**Why v1 gave no answer.** Audit reviews/AUDIT-goalserve-odds-vs-pm-result-2026-11-17.md: 30-match placebo gate unreachable (25 CS2, 12 Dota, 1 LoL matches), placebo not like-for-like (about 100 odds events per match), 54% of rows suspended so reopen prices read as late events, time-shift plant not recovered. v1 verdicts: unknown everywhere, nothing relayed as lead or no-lead.
**Deviation from STANDARD v1 sections 2-3 (onset/lead in seconds).** This test measures a signed mid change in fixed windows, not an onset time; the STANDARD bars (n>=30 events, +2 s, 60%) are replaced by bars V6 below. Nick's card approved this design in outline; the numbers in V6 are mine and are the part to read.
**Data.** Only Goalserve esports hour files `esports_2026111800` to `esports_2026112423` (UTC) and the PM recorder's own books/markets for the same period. Nothing earlier is used for the real run. The earlier files may be used for a smoke run only (exit code and counters, result never read).
**Rules** (code: feeds/goalserve_odds_vs_pm_v2.py, docstring V1-V8 is the authority; the table with line references is feeds/RULE-TO-CODE-goalserve-odds-vs-pm-v2-2026-11-18.md):
- V1 event: >= 2c move of the vig-removed home probability between two consecutive stored payloads, market valid and open in both. A payload with the market missing, suspended or invalid clears the baseline, so a reopen price is never an event. Markets: Match Winner (`Home/Away`) and Map N Winner (`Home/Away (map N)`) only. Cells = league prefix x kind.
- V2 at most 40 events per (match, kind), seeded random choice, number dropped reported.
- V3 R = (PM mid at t+60 minus mid at t) minus (mid at t-5 minus mid at t-60), on the rising team's OWN token (never inferred from the other token). Needs a mid at t-60 and a book row of either token within +-300 s.
- V4 placebo: same event times, 10 draws per event, random token and random sign. d = R_real minus mean placebo R.
- V5 cell statistic = mean over matches of the per-match mean d. 95% CI = match-clustered bootstrap, 2000 draws.
- V6 bar per cell: >= 12 matches, >= 60 usable events, mean d >= +0.005 (0.5c), CI lower bound > 0, and R_real above the placebo mean in >= 55% of events. CI upper bound < 0 = "PM moved first". Otherwise "no lead detected".
- V7 power check on the real series: a planted 1c edge on top of the cell's own noise (d - mean d + 0.01) must be detected by the same bar, else the cell verdict is "unknown (underpowered)". The report also prints min_detectable_effect.
- V9 capture outage (checker, 17 Nov): `--poll-log` is required. A payload whose preceding poll is more than 10 s earlier clears all baselines (counted), so the first price after an outage is never an event.
- V10 primary cells, named before the data: **cs2 Match Winner and dota2 Match Winner** (2 cells, no alpha correction). Every other cell is informational and cannot be called a pass (about 10 correlated cells would give about a 1-in-3 chance of a false pass; the checker's null simulation gives 3-4% per cell at 12-15 matches).
- V11 reporting: say "odds moved before the price within the 60 s window" or "did not", never "lead of N seconds" (windows cannot resolve under about 5 s). The power check (V7) is a power check only, not an edge-recovery check: pipeline correctness rests on the self test and on the hand check of `first20_events_biggest_cell` (t, rising team, token, R_real, placebo mean) before anything is relayed. d is volatility-matched, not momentum-matched. Report the tie count (`ties_d_zero`). The real run omits `--plant-c` and `--seed`; the audit checks plant_c 0.01 and seed 20261118 in the JSON.
- V8 seed 20261118. All verdicts are UNCHECKED until another project thread signs the rule-to-code table and an independent audit is done.
**What a result may say.** "meets bar", "no lead detected", "PM moved first" or "unknown", each with matches, events, mean d, CI, min_detectable_effect, and the sentence "bookmaker not identified (bm empty; vendor docs say bet365)". A negative or zero result is only a "no lead" if the power check passed.
**Known limits, stated now.** The windows (60 s) are wide relative to a 1-5 s lead: a lead below about 5 s is not separable from a PM move that is simply fast. Fixed windows do not give a lead in seconds; if the bar is met a timing follow-up is a separate locked test. Map-N alignment with PM is assumed. A PM move that happens to follow an odds move for another reason (shared news) counts as a lead.
**Self test** (offline, synthetic): feeds/test_goalserve_odds_vs_pm_v2.py, ALL PASS as pushed: trap events not counted, +15 s plant meets the bar (20 of 20 seeds), +10 s plant 20 of 20, no-edge false passes 0 of 20, PM-first world reads "PM moved first", own-book-only meets, other-book-only does not, cap and gate work.

- V12 Only data from 2026-11-18T00:00Z counts. One look each complete UTC day, up to 20 looks (19 Nov to 8 Dec, first look 19 Nov on 18 Nov's data). Look number K is read from the last hour file name, never an option; an incomplete day is ignored; K outside 1..20 stops the run.
- V17 Statistic and boundary (Reviewer E1(b), E1(c)). Per match: mean d over its usable events. Each primary cell uses the FIRST N_MAX matches by first usable event time (cs2 100, dota2 50; projected from the v1 accrual of 25 and 12 matches in 4 days), so later data never changes earlier matches. t-statistic of the per-match means with n-1 degrees of freedom, converted to z_eq with the same one-sided p. Information time t = matches used / N_MAX. Pass boundary on the Brownian scale: z_eq x sqrt(t) >= 2.60 (O'Brien-Fleming shape), i.e. z_eq >= 2.60/sqrt(t). By the reflection principle this holds for ANY look schedule (continuous monitoring bound): one-sided alpha 2(1 - Phi(2.60)) = 0.0093 per primary cell, so at most 0.019 over the two cells (alpha is split, not shared). Informational cells get no alpha and are never relayed as results. Boundary examples: 20 matches in cs2 (t 0.2): 5.81; 50 matches (t 0.5): 3.68; 100 matches: 2.60.
- V18 End-to-end plants every look, on that look's real data (Reviewer E1(a)), as raw book rows (Reviewer E1(a) point 1): per event and alone (so events do not contaminate each other's windows), a row in the recorder's own format with bid and ask shifted by the step from the last real row is built and passed through the same parse function and mid formula as every real book row, then through the same token lookup and windows. The plant token comes from a separate code path (outcome name to token dictionary) over the same markets file, so the plants catch code bugs but not a misordered outcomes/tokens pair in the markets file (V22). Faults that shift bid and ask equally (for example reading min instead of max of the bids) cancel in R and cannot be seen by any plant; they also do not matter for R. (P+) a +1c step at t+10 s on the rising token and -1c on the other token; (P-) the same step at t-10 s; (S) directions negated (the other token read as 'rising'); (SP) S plus P+. Required: the per-match mean of d(P+) - d(base) within 5% of +1c; d(P-) - d(base) within 5% of -1c; d(SP) - d(S) within 5% of -1c; and power: boundary x SE < 1c, SE = s/sqrt(n) of the per-match means. If any fails, every verdict except 'unknown (plant not recovered / underpowered at this look)' is withheld. The self test proves the check can fail: three mutants (wrong token read as rising; sign bug on the other token's plant; wrong pre-window start) are run and caught or changed.
- V21 Completeness (checker): the run stops, with no result, unless the poll log reaches 60 s past the end of day K, every hour file of days 1 to K exists for every hour in which the poll log shows capture running (hours with no poll are capture outages, listed in audit.hour_files), and no hour file is truncated. The cap and the placebo draws of an event are seeded from the event itself, so earlier looks reproduce on later looks.
- V22 One-time mapping audit before ANY pass is relayed to Nick: at least 20 cs2 and dota2 markets used by the test are compared with Polymarket's own gamma market (outcomes[i] with clobTokenIds[i], and that the team named outcomes[i] is the one whose Goalserve odds rose).
- V14 Verdicts. 'meets bar' = z_eq >= boundary and mean d >= 0.005 and share of events with R_real above the placebo mean >= 55% and >= 12 matches and >= 60 events and plants recovered. 'PM moved first' = z_eq <= -boundary. 'no lead detected' is decided only when the cell is closed (matches used reached N_MAX, or look 20) and the plants were recovered; before that the verdict is 'no pass yet (not a no-lead)'. A pass at any look stands and is audited; a later look never overturns it.
- V19 Daily output: look, matches used per cell, information time, boundary, z_eq, post and pre terms separately (real and placebo), per-cell excluded counts (including events with no own-book Polymarket mid), matches_by_day and cumulative, plain_summary, ties, outage counters, first20_events_biggest_cell. Another thread hand-checks the first 20 events before anything is relayed; independent audit before any pass is relayed to Nick.
- V20 Validation before the first look (self test, extra conditions switched off so only the boundary protects; the self-test output is the record). Null simulation of the whole 20-look schedule, 2,000 runs, accrual 5 and 2.5 matches a day: false pass per cell 0.6% (cs2) and 0.55% (dota2) with normal noise, 0.4% and 0.45% with heavy-tailed (t3) noise. The checker's 18-scenario grid (2, 3 and 6 matches a day; Poisson, overdispersed, back-loaded; normal and t3; 1,500 runs each): worst 0.87%. Target 1.25%. History: the first constant, 2.50, gave 1.20% in the worst scenario (too close to 1.25% given a Monte Carlo error of about 0.3%), so it was raised to 2.60 on 18 Nov. Power for a true edge of 0.6 standard deviations per match: passes by look 20 in 99.8% (cs2) and 91.8% (dota2) of runs. The percentile bootstrap of the earlier version is gone.
- Limits: the boundary assumes the t statistic of per-match means behaves like a normal martingale; the null simulation uses independent matches. A pass is still a claim about 'odds moved before the price within the 60 s window', not about seconds. Looks after 8 Dec are not pre-registered.
