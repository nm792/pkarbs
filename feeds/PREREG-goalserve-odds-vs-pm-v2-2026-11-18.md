**Data.** Only Goalserve esports hour files `esports_2026111800` to `esports_2026112423` (UTC) and the PM recorder's own books/markets for the same period. Nothing earlier is used for the real run. The earlier files may be used for a smoke run only (exit code and counters, result never read).
**Rules** (code: feeds/goalserve_odds_vs_pm_v2.py, docstring V1-V8 is the authority; the table with line references is feeds/RULE-TO-CODE-goalserve-odds-vs-pm-v2-2026-11-18.md):
- V1 event: >= 2c move of the vig-removed home probability between two consecutive stored payloads, market valid and open in both. A payload with the market missing, suspended or invalid clears the baseline, so a reopen price is never an event. Markets: Match Winner (`Home/Away`) and Map N Winner (`Home/Away (map N)`) only. Cells = league prefix x kind.
- V2 at most 40 events per (match, kind), seeded random choice, number dropped reported.
- V3 R = (PM mid at t+60 minus mid at t) minus (mid at t-5 minus mid at t-60), on the rising team's OWN token (never inferred from the other token). Needs a mid at t-60 and a book row of either token within +-300 s.
- V4 placebo: same event times, 10 draws per event, random token and random sign. d = R_real minus mean placebo R.
- V5 cell statistic = mean over matches of the per-match mean d. 95% CI = match-clustered bootstrap, 2000 draws.
- V6 bar per cell: >= 12 matches, >= 60 usable events, mean d >= +0.005 (0.5c), CI lower bound > 0, and R_real above the placebo mean in >= 55% of events. CI upper bound < 0 = "PM moved first". Otherwise "no lead detected".
- V7 power check on the real series: a planted 1c edge on top of the cell's own noise (d - mean d + 0.01) must be detected by the same bar, else the cell verdict is "unknown (underpowered)". The report also prints min_detectable_effect.
- V9 capture outage (checker, 17 Nov): `--poll-log` is required. A payload whose preceding poll is more than 10 s earlier clears all baselines (counted), so the first price after an outage is never an event.
- V10 primary cells, named before the data: **cs2 Match Winner and dota2 Match Winner** (2 cells, no alpha correction). Every other cell is informational and cannot be called a pass (about 10 correlated cells would give about a 1-in-3 chance of a false pass; the checker's null simulation gives 3-4% per cell at 12-15 matches).
- V11 reporting: say "odds moved before the price within the 60 s window" or "did not", never "lead of N seconds" (windows cannot resolve under about 5 s). The power check (V7) is a power check only, not an edge-recovery check: pipeline correctness rests on the self test and on the hand check of `first20_events_biggest_cell` (t, rising team, token, R_real, placebo mean) before anything is relayed. d is volatility-matched, not momentum-matched. Report the tie count (`ties_d_zero`). The real run omits `--plant-c` and `--seed`; the audit checks plant_c 0.01 and seed 20261118 in the JSON.
- V8 seed 20261118. All verdicts are UNCHECKED until another project thread signs the rule-to-code table and an independent audit is done.
**What a result may say.** "meets bar", "no lead detected", "PM moved first" or "unknown", each with matches, events, mean d, CI, min_detectable_effect, and the sentence "bookmaker not identified (bm empty; vendor docs say bet365)". A negative or zero result is only a "no lead" if the power check passed.
**Known limits, stated now.** The windows (60 s) are wide relative to a 1-5 s lead: a lead below about 5 s is not separable from a PM move that is simply fast. Fixed windows do not give a lead in seconds; if the bar is met a timing follow-up is a separate locked test. Map-N alignment with PM is assumed. A PM move that happens to follow an odds move for another reason (shared news) counts as a lead.
