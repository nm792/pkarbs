## Rule used (R-034)
No $/day without measured own-token depth plus volume at decision time, at $200 per trade or the nearest ask, $2,000/day cap. **FLOOR** = print-capped or hindsight backtest (a lower bound on capacity, not on edge). **MEASURED** = depth read from logged own-token books. **UNMEASURED** = no depth data exists; no $/day is stated. "Edge evidence" says whether the old drop rested on evidence of no edge or only on a broken, underpowered or unmeasured test.

Defect classes: **P** print-size cap; **D** unmeasured depth; **U** underpowered (power stated); **M** missing data; **B** broken or stopped recorder; **C** cost or method error; **N** none found (verdict stands on evidence of no edge).
