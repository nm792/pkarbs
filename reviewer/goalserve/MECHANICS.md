# MECHANICS: Goalserve in-play odds vs Polymarket sports/esports winner tokens (Goalserve thread, 20 Nov 2026)

Scope: the daily look (`feeds/run_daily_look.sh esports|tennis`). It compares when Goalserve odds move with when each Polymarket token's own book moves, in a +-60 s window. **It simulates no order, fill or P&L.** Steps 4-9 are written because the Reviewer's template asks for them and because any future paper trade would depend on them; none feeds a reported number today. Rule: every step is PUBLISHED (docs URL, fetched 20 Nov by curl, `.md` pages), MEASURED (file/commit, or live read by this thread on 20 Nov) or UNSOURCED.

## Contradictions between our code/findings and the docs (all listed)
1. **250 ms hold vs 1 s:** template step says 250 ms; docs and measurement say 250 ms is crypto/finance only, and sports use a seconds delay, 1 s measured. The look is unaffected (no fills).
2. **Esports fee 0.03 vs docs table Sports 0.05:** measured field is lower than the page. Use the market field, not the table, in any later cost term; the docs page may lag the field.
3. **"NO = 1 - YES" never used:** each token is read from its own book (SETTLED A1, Nick's 17 Nov rule); no docs contradiction found.
4. **Tennis suspensions:** v2 excludes post-point suspension moves; a tennis NO is "no pass on open-market moves", not "no Goalserve lead".

## What this implies for a rule (not a result)
- The look measures whether odds moved before the price within +-60 s (a yes/no per event). It cannot say "lead of N seconds", and nothing here supports an execution claim.
- A 1 s delay on a marketable order means a taker reacts to a stale book by up to 1 s plus unmeasured send latency. A lead below that is not tradeable by a taker; this needs the 100-GET RTT probe (PandaScore thread owns the RTT row) before any paper trade.
