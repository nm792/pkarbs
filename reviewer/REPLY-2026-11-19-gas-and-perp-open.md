- **Signal (the one that passed).** Follow rule: sign of (print D-1 minus print D-2) from `expiration_value` of the series; skip if |change| < 0.0005. Followed side = YES if up, NO if down.
- **Qualifying strike.** |floor_strike - print D-1| <= 0.03; mid in [0.25, 0.75] at the entry candle; ask > bid; cost of the followed side in (0.05, 0.95). Entry candle: 1, 6, 12 hours after open (the 14Z / 19Z / 01Z of the earlier grid), reported separately.

- **Size.** 10% of the $ traded on that strike in the 2 hours after entry (volume_fp x price x 0.5 taker share), capped at that share; no other size.
