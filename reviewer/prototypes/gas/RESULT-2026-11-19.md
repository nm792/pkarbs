## What the trade is
Polymarket lists three gas series, all settled on the AAA US national average regular price shown at gasprices.aaa.com at 10:00 ET:
1. **Daily** (`us-gas-price-daily`, since 9 Nov): six 1c buckets per day, opens 15:00Z the day before. Example: `us-gas-price-on-november-19-2026`.
2. **Weekly hit ladder** (`gas-price-hits-weekly`, since 18 Oct): "does AAA touch $X (↑) or $Y (↓) on any day Mon-Sun", 2c steps.
3. **Monthly hit ladder** (`gas-price-hits`, since March): the same over a calendar month, 5c steps. This is where the volume is.

Trade (Golden method applied to gas): nowcast the AAA print from what is already set (the previous day's print and the recent daily change, which PK arbs found beats Kalshi mids by 6-8 points, z 2.9-6.1) and buy a bucket's own token at its own ask when the model probability is above the ask plus fee. Hold to resolution. Fees on these markets: taker only, rate 0.04 x p(1-p) per share, maker rebate 25% (`feeSchedule` on each market).
