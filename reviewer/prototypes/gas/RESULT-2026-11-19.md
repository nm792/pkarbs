# Gas sizing prototype: result (Reviewer, 19 Nov 2026)

For Nick (asked 19 Nov 01:45Z that the Reviewer prototype this itself). Analysis only: no orders, no change to PK arbs code.

## Goal, position, the one thing that matters
- **Goal (Nick 18 Nov 08:18Z / 08:25Z):** size the gas edge "per day, small slice" against the pass bar of **$200/day reliably** (SETTLED A9; the CI lower bound decides).
- **Measured position:** on Polymarket, the venue we can trade, **gas does not clear $200/day, and no model can make it clear.** All Polymarket gas markets together trade **$1,645/day at prices of 0.05 to 0.95** (30-day window to 19 Nov 01:49Z). A 10% slice would need **122c of profit per $1 traded** to reach $200/day. Taking *all* of the qualifying flow would still need **12c per $**. Measured taker returns are near zero (+0.6c per $, CI -4.8 to +9.1, 21 events).
- **Verdict: FAIL (capacity) on Polymarket.** The size is only on Kalshi (about $205k/day national, about $419k/day across states, from PK arbs' own files), and Kalshi is out of scope (SETTLED A5, a fixed fact). Gas is closed.

## What the trade is
Polymarket lists three gas series, all settled on the AAA US national average regular price shown at gasprices.aaa.com at 10:00 ET:
1. **Daily** (`us-gas-price-daily`, since 9 Nov): six 1c buckets per day, opens 15:00Z the day before. Example: `us-gas-price-on-november-19-2026`.
2. **Weekly hit ladder** (`gas-price-hits-weekly`, since 18 Oct): "does AAA touch $X (↑) or $Y (↓) on any day Mon-Sun", 2c steps.
3. **Monthly hit ladder** (`gas-price-hits`, since March): the same over a calendar month, 5c steps. This is where the volume is.

Trade (Golden method applied to gas): nowcast the AAA print from what is already set (the previous day's print and the recent daily change, which PK arbs found beats Kalshi mids by 6-8 points, z 2.9-6.1) and buy a bucket's own token at its own ask when the model probability is above the ask plus fee. Hold to resolution. Fees on these markets: taker only, rate 0.04 x p(1-p) per share, maker rebate 25% (`feeSchedule` on each market).
