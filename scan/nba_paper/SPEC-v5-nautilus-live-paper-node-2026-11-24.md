## 3. Latency (fix 5)
SETTLED A3: non-weather markets hold marketable orders 250 ms; sports also carry their own secondsDelay. CLOB seconds_delay on NBA read 0 on 23 Nov (R-065 read, 2 games), so on NBA the A3 250 ms taker hold APPLIES; the 1 s delay applies to soccer, NHL, tennis and CS2 match markets (R-065 read). v4's "250 ms is not used for sports" was wrong for NBA and is withdrawn. Rule: hold = the market's seconds_delay if > 0, otherwise 250 ms, read per market at decision time (read failure = skip and log). A post-only maker order is not itself delayed (README C); the hold governs any taker leg and the 'order-eligible' time in the engine config: decision time + hold + order latency. Latency sensitivity cells: 0, 250 ms, 0.206 s (measured median, agent/outbox/nautilus-order-latency-2026-11-05.md, n=30), 1.281 s (p90), 1 s.
