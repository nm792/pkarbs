# Our own CS2 win model, built on the TheSports in-game captures and tested against the Polymarket price (10 Nov 2026)

## Data used
TheSports CS2 snapshot feed 6-10 Nov: 431,331 messages, 161 maps, per-player HP / alive / cash / ADR / kills, bomb plant, score, round clock. 102 maps paired to the Polymarket "Map N Winner" market by TEAM NAMES (84 usable after spread <= 6c and 90 s burn-in), 148 conditions of real prints from data-api /v2/trades (49,750 prints).
Older captures checked, and what they add: HLTV scorebot (3 matches, 2-3.5 min late, no usable in-round state), bo3.gg (round winners only, no in-round state), Pinnacle/OddsPapi (prices only), GRID (never captured, rejected). So in-round state exists only in the TheSports set; the older data can only test the score-level part.

## What we learned
1. The feed drops about 12% of its events (index gaps), so state must come from snapshots, not the kill list. The score ticks up at the moment of elimination, so rounds must be keyed by the feed round-start epoch, not by score (first build mislabelled rounds; fixed).
2. In-round state predicts the ROUND winner well. Held-out later half (80 maps, 5,418 samples): log-loss 0.592 vs 0.689 side-only. Drivers (home-perspective logit): alive diff +0.26 (less when few left), HP diff +0.43 per 100, bomb planted +0.66 for T fading with time after plant (-0.31), clock pressure +0.10, ADR diff +0.32, T side slightly worse than CT (T wins 47.7% of rounds at 5v5). 
3. At MAP level it adds almost nothing. Brier on all 84 maps: market 0.140, our model with state 0.157, old score-only model 0.157. Held-out half: market 0.143, model A 0.166, score-only 0.168.
4. Where the old (score-only) model mispriced vs the market: market logit minus old-model logit has sd 1.05; in-round state explains 0.3% to 1.7% of it (none of the state pieces significant). Updating team strength from the rounds played (prior sd 0.15 / 0.30 / 0.60 / 1.0) does not close it (Brier 0.1567 / 0.1577 / 0.1635 / 0.1695 vs 0.1568 fixed). It is not information timing: the market lagged by 90 s still beats the model (0.1536 vs 0.164).
5. So the market knows something we do not model. Candidates, none tested yet: individual player strength (who is alive), weapons/equipment value (weapon ids not mapped to prices), series context and earlier maps, roster/map form.

## Verdict
NO PROVEN EDGE. Best z is +1.5, the sign flips between halves (+20% test, -24% train), and the old score-only model does the same as the new one, so the in-round state adds nothing visible against the price. Variants tried: 3 models (A, C, S) x 2 thresholds x 2 sizes per-map, 2 per-round, 2 train, plus Bayes sd 0.15/0.60/1.0 for Brier only; with that many looks z of 1.5 is expected by chance. Power is low: 84 maps, 15-32 bets per cell.
Confidence 7/10 in "no edge from this model and data". Not covered: player-level strength, equipment value, series context, maps after 10 Nov (capture continues), match-winner markets, tier-1 only subset.

## Addendum 2 (10 Nov, 09:25Z): player strength from bo3.gg per-game stats (HLTV dropped)
bo3.gg `games/{id}/players_stats` gives per-game player_rating, ADR, KAST, kills/deaths and equipment value. Pulled 35,745 player-games from 2,021 matches (1 Jun to 10 Nov, teams in our paired maps; bo3_players_pull.py). Rating per player = mean of up to 20 prior games, standardised, shrunk toward the average (K=3); 74% of TheSports roster names matched a bo3 player (260 of 371 unique). Features: roster rating diff (pre-map) and alive-weighted rating diff (stars alive) per sample.
- Round log-loss (4-fold CV by match): 0.6059 vs 0.6063 base. Map Brier: 0.1532 vs 0.1531 base (market 0.1404). No gain.
- Fill test, all 84 maps out of fold, first real BUY print: 5c N=25 -0.8% (z -0.08), 5c N=100 +1.5% (+0.01), 10c N=25 +11.8% (+0.80), 10c N=100 -3.0% (-0.09), per-round 5c N=100 +3.3% (+0.45). With player + Elo + series: -0.3% / -1.3% / +2.6% / -3.2% / +3.9% (z -0.05 to +0.55). Oracle z +6.8 / +6.3.
- Equipment: bo3 equipment value is per game AFTER the game, so it cannot be a pre-game or in-round feature. The TheSports weapon-id dictionary (public docs confirm it needs the paid-trial key) is still the only route; Hermes task is in agent/inbox/cs2-weapon-dictionary-2026-11-10.md (PR #243 merged, so it is live).
- HLTV ratings: TinyFish fetch refused by the permission check as bot-check circumvention; dropped per coordinator.
Verdict unchanged: no proven edge. Player and series/team strength are exhausted (no gain beyond the price). Equipment is the only open candidate. Confidence 7/10.

## Addendum 3 (10 Nov, 12:5xZ): equipment value, using the TheSports weapon dictionary Hermes pulled (rn1/scores/thesports/reference/csgo-weapons.json, 67 weapons)
Weapon ids mapped to names (ak47 33%, m4a1-silencer 15%, awp 11%, m4a1 10%, glock 5% of alive-player snapshots) and priced with standard CS2 buy prices. Features: value of the main weapons of each team's ALIVE players (equipment at risk right now), team main-weapon value 20-40 s after round start (post-buy), and that start diff by side (equip_feats.py, cv_equip.py).
- Round log-loss (4-fold CV by match, all maps): 0.5885 vs 0.6063 base. Equipment is REAL signal for who wins the round.
- It does not carry to the map price: map Brier 0.1537 vs 0.1531 base (market 0.1404), because the map price barely moves with one round's equipment and the DP assumes constant strength for later rounds.
- Fill test, all 84 maps out of fold (first real BUY print, placebo from other maps): +EQUIP 5c N=25 +5.4% z +0.44; 5c N=100 -3.2% z -0.22; 10c N=25 +13.6% z +0.84; 10c N=100 +0.3% z +0.06; per-round 5c N=100 +6.2% z +0.79. With equipment + player + Elo + series: +7.5% z +0.62 / -3.7% z -0.16 / +13.7% z +0.88 / -7.0% z -0.35 / +6.1% z +0.81. Oracle z +6.8 / +6.3.
- Verdict: all three candidates (player strength, series/team strength, equipment) are exhausted; none passes. No proven edge. Best z is +0.88 across 13 variants of this addendum alone. Confidence 7/10.
- Not modelled, and where a gain could still hide: equipment persistence into FUTURE rounds (economy/loss-bonus carry), grenades and armor (not in the feed's weapon field), round-level markets. GameScorekeeper (feeds thread) documents per-round equipment_state and 10 s full_state in a free 14-day trial; this repo would need that only if equipment-carry proves worth modelling.
