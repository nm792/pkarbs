# Our own CS2 win model, built on the TheSports in-game captures and tested against the Polymarket price (10 Nov 2026)

## Data used
TheSports CS2 snapshot feed 6-10 Nov: 431,331 messages, 161 maps, per-player HP / alive / cash / ADR / kills, bomb plant, score, round clock. 102 maps paired to the Polymarket "Map N Winner" market by TEAM NAMES (84 usable after spread <= 6c and 90 s burn-in), 148 conditions of real prints from data-api /v2/trades (49,750 prints).
Older captures checked, and what they add: HLTV scorebot (3 matches, 2-3.5 min late, no usable in-round state), bo3.gg (round winners only, no in-round state), Pinnacle/OddsPapi (prices only), GRID (never captured, rejected). So in-round state exists only in the TheSports set; the older data can only test the score-level part.

## What we learned
1. The feed drops about 12% of its events (index gaps), so state must come from snapshots, not the kill list. The score ticks up at the moment of elimination, so rounds must be keyed by the feed round-start epoch, not by score (first build mislabelled rounds; fixed).
2. In-round state predicts the ROUND winner well. Held-out later half (80 maps, 5,418 samples): log-loss 0.592 vs 0.689 side-only. Drivers (home-perspective logit): alive diff +0.26 (less when few left), HP diff +0.43 per 100, bomb planted +0.66 for T fading with time after plant (-0.31), clock pressure +0.10, ADR diff +0.32, T side slightly worse than CT (T wins 47.7% of rounds at 5v5). 
3. At MAP level it adds almost nothing. Brier on all 84 maps: market 0.140, our model with state 0.157, old score-only model 0.157. Held-out half: market 0.143, model A 0.166, score-only 0.168.
4. Where the old (score-only) model mispriced vs the market: market logit minus old-model logit has sd 1.05; in-round state explains 0.3% to 1.7% of it (none of the state pieces significant). Updating team strength from the rounds played (prior sd 0.15 / 0.30 / 0.60 / 1.0) does not close it (Brier 0.1567 / 0.1577 / 0.1635 / 0.1695 vs 0.1568 fixed). It is not information timing: the market lagged by 90 s still beats the model (0.1536 vs 0.164).
5. So the market knows something we do not model. Candidates, none tested yet: individual player strength (who is alive), weapons/equipment value (weapon ids not mapped to prices), series context and earlier maps, roster/map form.

## Verdict
NO PROVEN EDGE. Best z is +1.5, the sign flips between halves (+20% test, -24% train), and the old score-only model does the same as the new one, so the in-round state adds nothing visible against the price. Variants tried: 3 models (A, C, S) x 2 thresholds x 2 sizes per-map, 2 per-round, 2 train, plus Bayes sd 0.15/0.60/1.0 for Brier only; with that many looks z of 1.5 is expected by chance. Power is low: 84 maps, 15-32 bets per cell.
Confidence 7/10 in "no edge from this model and data". Not covered: player-level strength, equipment value, series context, maps after 10 Nov (capture continues), match-winner markets, tier-1 only subset.
